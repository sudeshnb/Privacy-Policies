# Privacy Policy for Bizz

_Last Updated: May 10, 2026_

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Bizz.

Bizz is a vegetable shop management application designed to help manage inventory, sales, expenses, and stock operations efficiently.

We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our users and their business data. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected within the application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

As a shop management application, Bizz primarily processes business-related information, including:

### Inventory Data
- Product names (e.g., ගෝවා, Cauliflower)
- Stock quantities
- Buying prices
- Selling prices
- Batch information
- Wastage records

### Transaction Data
- Sales records
- Purchase history
- Expense entries
- Supplier details
- Purchase IDs

### Device Information
We may collect limited technical information such as:
- Device model
- Operating system version
- App version
- Crash logs

This information is used only to improve app performance and stability.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

The collected information is used strictly for business management purposes, including:

- Managing stock and inventory
- Calculating remaining quantities
- FIFO stock tracking
- Monitoring profit margins
- Tracking wastage and damaged goods
- Generating reports and analytics
- Improving application performance

---

## 4. Data Storage and Security

All application data is stored securely using cloud database technologies such as Firebase services.

We take reasonable security measures to protect business information from:
- Unauthorized access
- Data loss
- Misuse
- Modification

We do not sell, rent, or trade your business data to third parties.

Access to the application should only be granted to authorized personnel.
